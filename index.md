## What the Pager and Producer do

The Producer runs the plan. The Pagers tell everyone what to do next.

<section class="section" id="sec-how-it-works" markdown="1">

## How it works

The Producer talks directly to the pagers on your network. Nothing goes through the developer.

{% include how-it-works.html %}

If a Pager cannot find the Producer on its own, for example on a hosted Producer, enter its address as `host` or `host:port`. The default port is 13380.

</section>
