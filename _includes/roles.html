Everyone gets a pager

Pick a role and the pager shows only what that role needs, readable from across the stage. Portrait, landscape or a lower third over your own screen.

Phone or iPad

STANDBY and GO in big colour, the item and its countdown, what’s next, and your notes.

ESP32 hardware

A small dedicated pager for crew who would rather not hold a phone on stage. (Mac pager at the ESP32’s screen size)

Lower third

A slim strip with the time, the item and its countdown over your own picture, for an Apple TV or a confidence monitor. STANDBY and GO take over when they’re called.
